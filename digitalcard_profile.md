## Tagline / promise
Create and share your digital card in minutes. Update anytime. Track every scan.

## Core features (lead with these)
1. **Build in minutes** — no design skills; create from your phone
2. **Premium templates** — conversion-optimized professional looks
3. **Custom branding** — logo, colors, social links
4. **Smart QR sharing** — scan to connect; recipient does not need the app
5. **One-tap save to contacts** — details saved to the phone contact book
6. **Instant share** — QR, WhatsApp, or direct link
7. **WhatsApp Connect** — built-in WhatsApp so clients message you instantly
8. **Live analytics** — card views, WhatsApp clicks, QR scans, phone clicks
9. **Contact management** — organize connections in one place
10. **Instant updates** — change phone, email, or role once; the card stays current everywhere
11. **Secure and private** — encrypted data; you control visibility
12. **Cost savings** — stop reprinting paper cards that get thrown away

## How it works (4 steps — reuse in carousels)
1. Download the app (iOS or Android)
2. Create profile / sign up
3. Build card (pick template, add details)
4. Share and track (QR, link, WhatsApp + analytics)
